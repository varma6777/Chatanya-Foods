Welcome to

Chaitanya Food Court

In 2008, Chaitanya's Food Court opened its doors in Kukatpally with a mission to bring quality South and North Indian vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes to the people of Hyderabad. We saw an opportunity blend our cherished cultural heritage, featuring recipes with Ulavacharu, Sambar, and Avakaya pickles harmoniously fused with the rich taste of Nizam’s cuisine. We serve India's rich diversity of regional cuisines to those yearning for the authentic tastes of home while dining out.
